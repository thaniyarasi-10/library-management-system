2. Membership Terms and Conditions
  1. The membership is issued to the registered user and is non-transferable.
  2. The member is responsible for all books borrowed using their membership account.
  3. Borrowed books must be returned within the applicable due date.
  4. The member may renew borrowed books subject to the library's renewal rules and book availability.
  5. The member can view their borrowing history and current due dates through the Library Management System.
  6. The member must keep their account credentials secure and must not allow another person to use their account.
  7. The library reserves the right to cancel or suspend membership in case of violation of library rules or misuse of library services.
  8. Membership access is valid only until the stated expiry date unless cancelled earlier in accordance with library policies.
